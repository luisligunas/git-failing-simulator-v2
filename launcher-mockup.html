Dipstick basics

What you're actually buying

A dipstick is our flagship quick-turn study: a fast, confident read on a single question. Think of it as dipping a measuring stick into the market: you get a clear level back without commissioning a full research project.

Each dipstick is a fixed bundle: 5 questions, 500 respondents, 5 demographic filters, with live tracking, an AI insight deck, and a cross-tabulated Excel export. It's perfect for concept checks, pricing sanity tests, packaging shortlists, and pulse reads, typically back in about three days.

Reach for a dipstick when 5 questions and 500 general-population respondents will settle the call. For example, "Which of these 3 sinigang sachets do shoppers prefer?" or "Is ₱29 the right price for this new milk tea?"

Build a custom study when you need a bigger sample, a longer survey, a niche audience, or deeper analysis. The advanced calculator on the pricing page prices these instantly.

Yes. Every study has a Live tracking view that updates as completes land: overall completion rate, fieldwork pace, and quota fill by segment. You can pause and resume fieldwork at any time. Full insights unlock the moment your last response is collected.

Pricing & the calculator

The dipstick bundle, the model, and the +30% rule

The dipstick is a fixed, all-in bundle at ₱50,000, VAT inclusive. That single price covers survey-design help, full fieldwork to 500 respondents, live tracking, the AI insight deck, and the cross-tab Excel. There are no per-seat or per-export add-ons.

Because it's a fixed package, the dipstick is not priced through the calculator; the calculator is for fully custom studies (see below).

For anything beyond the dipstick bundle, the advanced calculator on the pricing page gives you an instant, itemised quote using Hustle PH's quantitative pricing model. It starts from a base respondent cost (sample size × cost-per-complete for your audience) and then layers multipliers:

  • Survey length, geography, incidence rate, and timeline
  • Open-ended question premium and data-quality tier
  • Quota-control and reporting-package add-ons

It then adds 12% VAT and shows the total. Every line is broken out so you can see exactly what each choice costs.

Most audiences (general population, grocery shoppers, GCash users, parents, students, higher SECs) are already profiled in our panel, so we can field to them directly at standard rates.

When your audience isn't already on file and we need to source it specially (for example, owners of a specific appliance brand, or people who shopped a niche category last week), a flat +30% surcharge applies on top of the study subtotal. The calculator and the targeting step both flag this clearly before you commit, so there are never surprise costs at checkout.

The calculator gives a firm estimate, and for standard studies it's confirmed as-is at checkout. For very low-incidence or heavily sourced audiences, our team may sanity-check feasibility before fieldwork starts, but we'll always confirm the number with you first. The dipstick bundle is fixed and never changes.

Data quality & the panel

Who answers, and how we keep it clean

Every response comes from the Hustle PH panel, over 1.8 million verified Filipino respondents nationwide, spanning NCR, the rest of Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao, across SEC ABC1 down to DE. These are real, profiled people who earn for completing studies on the Hustle app, not bought-in traffic.

Quality control runs throughout fieldwork:

  • Identity-verified panellists, one account per real person
  • Attention and consistency checks plus speeder/straight-lining detection
  • Open-ended responses screened for gibberish and copy-paste
  • Photo/receipt tasks retain metadata for authenticity validation

Flagged completes are replaced at no cost so your final sample is clean.

At the dipstick's national sample of 500, you're working at roughly a ±4.4% margin of error at 95% confidence, more than enough to call a clear winner between concepts or read directional preference. For tighter sub-group reads or smaller effects, scale the sample up in the calculator.

Turnaround time

From launch to deck

A standard national dipstick fills in about 2–3 days, and your AI insight deck plus Excel export generate automatically the moment the last response lands. Fieldwork usually starts within hours of payment.

Fill speed depends on how narrow your audience is, how strict your quotas are, the survey length, and the incidence rate (how common your target is in the population). A broad NCR Gen-Z study fills fastest; a low-incidence niche in a single province takes longer. The calculator's timeline option lets you pay to prioritise a rush study.

Yes. In the custom calculator, set the timeline to Urgent or Rush to put your study at the front of the field queue. If you're on a tight launch window, book a meeting and we'll confirm feasibility before you pay.

Targeting

Reaching exactly the right people

Out of the box you can slice by age, gender, region, SEC, and category behaviour, plus ready-made segments like supermarket shoppers, online shoppers, parents/decision-makers, e-wallet users, students, and higher SECs (ABC1). The dipstick includes up to 5 demographic filters; custom studies have no cap.

We can source niche audiences that aren't already profiled, for instance "bought a specific noodle brand in the last 7 days" or "owns an EV in Cebu." Because that requires special sourcing, the +30% custom-targeting surcharge applies (see Pricing). The builder flags any custom segment and shows the surcharge before you commit.

Yes. Quota controls let you lock in the mix you need, for example 50/50 male/female, or even spread across NCR, Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. The live tracker shows quota fill per segment so you can see when each cell is full. Quota controls are a calculator add-on for custom studies; the dipstick uses balanced national defaults.

Exports & reports

What you walk away with
  • AI insight deck (.pptx): a presentation-ready story with charts, key findings, and recommendations
  • Cross-tabulated Excel (.xlsx): every question cut by every demographic
  • Ask-your-data chatbot: drill into drivers, cross-cuts, and verbatims in plain language
  • Raw response data for your own analysis

Absolutely. The deck downloads as a standard editable .pptx: re-order slides, restyle, and drop in your own branding. The chatbot is great for pulling an extra cut or a supporting verbatim while you polish it for the boardroom.

Completed studies stay in your dashboard indefinitely. Re-download the deck or Excel, and keep asking the chatbot questions, any time after fieldwork closes. Nothing expires.

Vouchers & credits

Discounts and prepaid balance

Enter your code in the Voucher field at checkout and the discount applies to that study's total before you pay. Some vouchers are multi-use across several studies, and the checkout shows how many uses you have left (e.g. "3 of 5 uses remaining").

Credit is a prepaid balance (from a top-up, a promo, or a goodwill adjustment) shown in your top bar. It's applied automatically toward your next study's total before any other payment method is charged. Credit doesn't expire.

Yes. Teams running studies regularly can arrange a bundle of prepaid credits at a discount. Book a meeting and we'll put together a plan that fits your research calendar.

Account roles & sharing

Working as a team

Yes. Invite colleagues into a shared workspace so everyone sees the same studies, drafts, and results. Great for a brand team where the manager, an analyst, and a category lead all need visibility into the same dipstick.

  • Admin: manages the workspace, billing, and members
  • Editor: builds studies, sets targeting, and launches (with billing permission)
  • Viewer: reads live tracking and insights, no edit or launch rights

You can download the deck and Excel to share however you like, and the insight deck is a standalone .pptx anyone can open. Read-only links for live tracking can be shared with stakeholders who don't need an account.

Payment methods

How you pay to launch

You can pay with a credit or debit card (Visa / Mastercard), GCash, or a voucher / prepaid credit. Enterprise clients can also arrange invoiced bank transfer, handy for procurement-driven brand teams.

Building and pricing studies is completely free; you're only charged when you launch a study to the field at checkout. Fieldwork begins right after payment is confirmed.

Yes. Every study generates a VAT-inclusive official receipt under Agile Data Solutions Inc., available from Billing & invoices. For procurement, we can issue a proforma invoice ahead of payment. Just message our team.
